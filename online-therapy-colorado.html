Online therapy · anywhere in Colorado

Therapy from wherever you are in Colorado.

Every session I offer is by secure video. If you're in Colorado, you can work with me, whether you live in Denver or three hours from it.

Book the free 15-minute call
Where clients are

Clients log on from all over the state.

I see individuals and couples across Colorado: the Denver metro, Colorado Springs, Fort Collins, Boulder, and small towns where the nearest trauma-informed therapist is a long drive away, if there's one at all.

Inside the state, distance stops mattering. You get the same trauma-informed work whether you're ten minutes from downtown Denver or on the Western Slope.

How it works

A link, a quiet spot, and fifty minutes.

Before each session you'll get a secure, confidential video link. There's nothing to download.

Find somewhere you won't be overheard. That might be a bedroom with the door shut, a parked car on your lunch break, or a walk with headphones. If privacy at home is complicated, tell me and we'll work around it.

Individual sessions run 50 minutes and couples sessions run 60. Most people meet weekly or every other week.

Couples can join from the same couch or from two different towns.

Does it work?

Video therapy isn't the lesser version.

For most concerns, research finds outcomes for individual and couples therapy over video broadly comparable to sessions in an office. Some people do better online. Being in your own space can lower your guard in a way an unfamiliar office never quite does.

I wrote more about who it suits, and what to do when home isn't private, in Is Telehealth Therapy Right for You?
