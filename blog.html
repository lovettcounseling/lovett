About This Journal

Written for the space between sessions.

These posts cover the questions that come up most in a first session, and the ones people search at eleven at night: what trauma-informed care means in practice, how to tell burnout from a hard stretch, why the same argument keeps coming back, and whether therapy over video holds up.

Nothing here replaces working with someone. It's meant to be useful on its own, and to make the decision about reaching out a little less opaque. If something lands and you'd like to talk it through, the three ways to work with me are laid out plainly, or you can send a note.
