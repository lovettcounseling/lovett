Services built around where you actually are.

Individual therapy, couples therapy, and consulting for the people who support others — all trauma-informed, all collaborative, all paced to fit real life.

Every practice looks a little different. Here's a plain look at the three ways to work with Whitney — who each is built for, what a session actually costs, and how to know where to start.

i.

Individual Therapy

One-on-one, trauma-informed therapy for adults carrying anxiety, depression, burnout, trauma, or a season of life that's harder than it should be. Sessions are paced to what your nervous system can actually hold — no worksheets, no script, just real, collaborative work toward feeling more like yourself.

$175 per session·50 minutes·sliding scale available

ii.

Couples Therapy

For partners at any stage — newly struggling or years into the same argument — who want to actually understand each other again. We slow down the pattern, name what's underneath it, and build communication that holds up outside the therapy room, not just inside it.

$200 per session·60 minutes·partners welcome at any stage

iii.

Consulting & Training

Trauma-informed training and organizational consulting for schools, nonprofits, healthcare teams, and helping professionals — covering burnout, compassion fatigue, staff support, and the practices that let people do hard work sustainably.

By inquiry·in-person in Denver or virtual·single trainings or ongoing engagements
