Common Questions

Sorting out where to start.

If you're working on something for yourself, start with individual therapy. If the difficulty lives between you and a partner, start with couples therapy. If you're responsible for a team carrying hard work, consulting and training is the right door. A free 15-minute call sorts it out quickly.

I'm an out-of-network provider and don't bill insurance directly. I provide monthly superbills you can submit to your plan for possible out-of-network reimbursement, and a limited number of sliding-scale slots are available.

Therapy is delivered by secure telehealth to anyone located in Colorado. Consulting and training is available in person around Denver and the Front Range, or virtually for organizations anywhere in the country.

Not sure which fits?

That's what the free 15-minute call is for: a short conversation to figure out the right starting point together.
