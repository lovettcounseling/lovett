Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information this website collects when you visit, and how it's used. It covers the website only. It is separate from, and does not replace, the HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices you receive directly as a therapy client, which governs your protected health information and clinical records.

Information This Site Collects

When you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide, so we can respond to your inquiry. We do not require you to submit sensitive clinical details through the form, and we ask that you not include them.

Like most websites, this site also uses Google Analytics to understand general traffic patterns — pages visited, time on site, and approximate location by city or region. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

Cookies

This site uses cookies associated with Google Analytics to distinguish visitors and measure site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; doing so will not affect your ability to use the site or contact us.

How Information Is Used

Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to begin the intake process. Analytics data is used only to understand and improve the site. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This site links to third-party services, including Instagram and Psychology Today, and uses Google Analytics and a third-party form-processing service to handle contact submissions. Those services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data, which we encourage you to review.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 through the site.

Your Choices

You can decline to submit the contact form, disable cookies in your browser, or use a browser extension that blocks analytics tracking. None of these affect your ability to reach out by phone or email instead.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the site or applicable law. The effective date above reflects the most recent update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to lovettcounselingandconsulting@gmail.com.
